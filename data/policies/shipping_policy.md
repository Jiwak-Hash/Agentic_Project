# Shipping policy

These are fictional policies for DemoCart, a demo e-commerce company.

- Standard delivery generally takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.
- Delivery estimates are not guarantees; carrier or weather delays may occur.
- Contact customer support if delivery exceeds the estimated window.
- Damaged or incorrect deliveries must be escalated to customer support for manual review, with the order ID, a description, and photos.
