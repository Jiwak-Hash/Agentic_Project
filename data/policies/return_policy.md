# Return policy

These are fictional policies for DemoCart, a demo e-commerce company.

- Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.
- For an ordinary return, the product must be unused, with its original packaging and accessories.
- Contact customer support to request a return. Approval follows a review of eligibility and inspection of the returned item.
- If a product arrives damaged or incorrect, contact customer support with the order ID, a description, and photos. The case must be escalated for manual review; do not assume the ordinary unused-item rule resolves these cases.
- Eligible refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after refund approval.
