# Cancellation policy

These are fictional policies for DemoCart, a demo e-commerce company.

- Orders can be cancelled only while their status is `Processing`.
- Orders with status `Shipped` or `Delivered` cannot be directly cancelled. Other statuses are not eligible for direct cancellation either.
- After delivery, customers may request a return within 7 days of delivery, subject to the return policy.
- Contact customer support to request an eligible cancellation. This FAQ cannot check order status or perform cancellations.
- Damaged or incorrect products should be escalated for manual review rather than directly cancelled after shipment.
