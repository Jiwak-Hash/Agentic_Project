# Refund policy

These are fictional policies for DemoCart, a demo e-commerce company.

- Refunds are available for eligible returns requested within 7 days of delivery.
- After a returned item is inspected and the refund is approved, the refund is processed within 5–7 business days after approval.
- Refunds for prepaid orders go to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, support arranges the refund method during manual review.
- Damaged or incorrect products require manual review by customer support before a return or refund decision. A refund is not guaranteed before review.
- This FAQ explains policy only; it cannot approve or issue refunds.
